Before we start

Are you using AI today?

I open with three show-of-hands questions. Click each one to see what I expected the room to say.

My assumptionYou do not have access to enterprise AI. At most, some very limited Copilot. There is no official AI tool for prosecutors, and I built this CLE on that assumption.
Your Honor, I’d like to enter a plea

Guilty. Of not knowing exactly what I’m doing up here.

I’ve been a prosecutor, I’ve studied ethical AI in prosecution, and I work on legal AI every day. I still don’t have all the answers, because nobody does.

Mitigating factors

There are no experts.

AI changes drastically every few weeks. Anyone who tells you they have it figured out is describing last month.

Request for relief

Object loudly.

  • Tell me how your office actually handles discovery, charging, and trial prep.
  • Chime in with ideas and suggestions for ways to use AI.
  • Share what you’ve tried with AI, good or bad.
