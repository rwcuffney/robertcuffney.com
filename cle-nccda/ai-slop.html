    The cure

    Four habits that keep slop out of your filings.

    • Hunt for nouns you can check.

      Real writing names a defendant, a date, a statute, a page. If you can't verify a sentence against anything, it's probably filler.

    • Give the tool the facts, then ask for less.

      Paste in what you actually know (and are allowed to share) and ask for a short draft. Vague prompts get vague output.

    • Cut any sentence that could appear in any brief.

      If a line would fit just as well in a DWI motion as in a homicide brief, it isn't doing any work in either.

    • Read every citation in the source before you sign.

      Pull the case, find the quote on the page, confirm the pin cite. Your signature certifies it, not the tool's.
